# Cleveland Museum of Art

The palettes of the [Cleveland Museum of Art](https://www.clevelandart.org)'s
paintings and prints that the museum releases under CC0 through its
[Open Access](https://www.clevelandart.org/open-access) program and API.

- `works.json` and `works.csv`: one entry per work (see the
  [format](../README.md#format)).
- Titles, artists, dates and links are the museum's, as its API gives them.
- Images aren't copied here: `image` points to the museum's own server.
- A work the museum no longer offers under CC0 is removed at the next update.

Data: Cleveland Museum of Art, Open Access, CC0. This project isn't
affiliated with or endorsed by the museum.
